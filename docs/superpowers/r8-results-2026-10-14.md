## Observations
- Similarity to recent lines rose (maxsim >=.5 went from 1% to 6%). That is expected, since short reactions like "lol same" and "lol yeah" naturally repeat. Watch that it doesn't turn into a tic.
- IRL fell to 2% because the chat-log prompt rarely invites real-life asides. Raise it with the R3 knob or a life-aside shape if wanted.
- Some replies are still muddled or land in the wrong channel ("Lol u mad or what lmao" in LFG). The model also still invents post-vanilla content ("dailies"); that is R2, the next slice.
- Profanity is intentionally not filtered (user preference).
- During development the agent's worktree sat under `.claude/worktrees/` inside the live checkout, which triggered 35 harmless live reloads. Put future worktrees outside the live folder.
