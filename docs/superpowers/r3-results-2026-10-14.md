## What changed
- `irl_identity.py` gives each bot a deterministic person. Slots are seeded from the bot's name: age, occupation, household (with a fixed partner gender and name), two life details, and an optional signature phrase. Archetype `typing_style`s no longer contain quoted catchphrases.
- The `irl_json` column holds the composed identity. Existing bots migrate lazily the first time they are loaded. Pinned eval casts are never rewritten.
- `irl_budget.py` keeps a fleet-wide rolling window (200 lines, 15% target). Its `is_irl_line` classifier is shared by the budget and the metric. When the budget is spent, /tick and /heard drop the life details, the IRL banter activity and IRL moods, and add a "stay in the game world" rule. Whispers are never budgeted.
- `postfilter.py` strips emoji, enforces a length cap (140 for ticks, 200 for heard), drops mashed bot-name tokens, and detects third-person narration. Narration gets one re-roll and is then silenced, with at most 2 LLM calls per line.
- `eval/chatter_metrics.py` is the tracked before/after tool. It exits with an error if the `--after` marker is missing.
